[SAMPLE] Cabinet approves draft amendment to Bangladesh Labour Act

[SAMPLE — fictional article for offline testing] The cabinet on Thursday approved the draft of the Bangladesh Labour (Amendment) Act 2026 at its weekly meeting chaired by the head of government.

Under the draft, paid maternity leave will be increased from 112 days to 120 days for workers in all registered factories.

The draft lowers the threshold for forming a trade union from 20 percent of workers to 15 percent in a single establishment.

The cabinet secretary told reporters that the bill will be placed before parliament in its next session after vetting by the Law Ministry.

A labour rights group said the changes were "a step forward but not enough", while an employers' association said it needed time to study the draft.
